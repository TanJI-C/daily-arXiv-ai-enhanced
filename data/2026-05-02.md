We evaluate our methods on several real-world and synthetic datasets and queries, and the results show ours consistently achieve good speedup and, in extreme cases, up to 3x speedup and 98708x speedup, when compared to index-assisted uniform sampling and classic scan-based stratified sampling respectively.
