Abstract: In semantic query engines, predicates are evaluated by machine-learned models, and the choice of a query plan affects not only the cost of a query but also its result. We give a formal problem definition for cost-accuracy optimization of such queries. Our starting point is the calibrated confidence that decision models such as Jev attach to each decision.
