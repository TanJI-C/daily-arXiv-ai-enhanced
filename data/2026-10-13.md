Abstract: Scalable logic-based analytics are frequently dominated by cyclic multi-way joins over large relational datasets. State-of-the-art systems evaluate these joins through chains of binary joins, incurring substantial overhead from large intermediate relations that must be materialized and redistributed across the network. Worst-Case Optimal Joins (WCOJ) offer a provably superior alternative by evaluating joins one variable at a time, eliminating intermediate blowup.
