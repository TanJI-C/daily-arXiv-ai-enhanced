Executing such operators efficiently is challenging because LLM comparisons are expensive, stochastic, and sometimes inconsistent. Pairwise approaches require many costly calls. Listwise approaches such as external merge sort rank several items per call, but they follow a fixed merge schedule and keep no record of earlier answers: each merge call re-asks orderings that previous calls have already revealed, directly or by transitivity.
